Services

The most common jobs in Berlin homes. If yours isn't listed, ask anyway.

Furniture assembly
IKEA and other flat-pack furniture, kitchen units, loose handles, hinges and cupboard doors.
Mounting & hanging
Shelves, pictures, mirrors, curtain rails and TV wall brackets.
Small plumbing jobs
Taps, siphons, seals, toilet cisterns and dripping fittings, where appropriate.
Painting & walls
Painting walls, doors and furniture, filling holes, skirting boards.
Doors & windows
Door adjustments, locks and fittings, draught seals, sticking windows.
Sealing & grout
Re-sealing bathrooms and kitchens, renewing silicone, repairing tile grout.
Balcony & outdoors
Balcony furniture, privacy screens, letterboxes, bike racks, small woodwork.
General DIY & odd jobs
Minor household repairs and maintenance, including for landlords and property managers.
What I don't do

No work on gas pipes or gas appliances, no fixed electrical installation (fuse boxes, wiring, new sockets). That needs a licensed specialist, and I'll tell you so honestly instead of doing it halfway.

FAQ

Which areas do you cover?

Berlin. Send your district or postcode with your message and I'll confirm.

Do I need to buy materials myself?

Materials and any agreed additional costs are charged on top of the labour. We agree what's needed before work starts.

How do I book?

Message me with a short description of the job, your location and a few photos. I'll give you an estimate before arranging a visit.

When do I pay?

On completion.

What if it turns out to be a bigger problem?

I tell you immediately and agree the price with you before I continue. For gas or electrics I'll point you to a licensed specialist.
